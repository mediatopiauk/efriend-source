5 · Focus management

WCAG AA accessibility contract

Identical contract to the modal system. Anything different per-drawer is documented per-drawer in the showcase.

  • · On open, focus moves to first focusable inside .d-body (skip the close button).
  • · Focus is trapped inside .d while open. Tab cycles within.
  • · On close, focus returns to the triggering element.
  • · Every text / background pair inside the drawer must clear WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large).
  • · Close button has aria-label="Close"; drawer root has role="dialog" aria-modal="true".
  • · Screen-reader announcement reads the lens chip + title on open.
