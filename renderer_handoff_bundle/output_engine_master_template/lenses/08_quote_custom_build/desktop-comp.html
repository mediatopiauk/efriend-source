— Frequently Asked —

The questions we get most.

Do you do one-off projects, or is there a minimum?

+
No minimum. We'll happily cut a single piece. The smallest invoice we've sent this year was £38 (a brass house number). The largest was £142,000 (a facade). We treat both the same.

What file formats do you accept?

+
For 2D cutting: DXF, DWG, AI, PDF (vector). For 3D: STEP, IGES, STL. We can also work from photos, scans, or pencil sketches — we'll digitise it for you (small charge applies).

How much will my project cost?

+
Depends on material, complexity, finish, and quantity. Send us the brief and we'll come back with a fixed price within a working day. No hidden charges, no "from" pricing — what you're quoted is what you pay.

What's your typical lead time?

+
For simple cutting jobs: 3–5 working days. For finished or anodised work: 7–10 days. For architectural projects: 4–8 weeks. Rush work possible — phone us and we'll see what we can do.

Can you deliver outside the Midlands?

+
Yes — UK-wide, next-day on most items. Pallet delivery for larger pieces. International by arrangement (we've shipped to France, Germany, and the US).

Do you sign NDAs?

+
Yes, no fuss. We'll sign yours, or use our standard one. Most projects don't need it but designers and inventors often appreciate the option.
