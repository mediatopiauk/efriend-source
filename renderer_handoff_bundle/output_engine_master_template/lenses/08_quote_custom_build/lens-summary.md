# Lens 8 — Quote / Custom Build

For configurable services where every job is bespoke and the customer can't just "add to basket" — CNC routing, laser cutting, sign-making, bespoke joinery, fabrication, architectural metalwork, custom printers, monumental masons. Shopify can't serve them and Local Authority undersells them. The page is built around a serious **quote-request form with file upload** and a process explainer that converts uncertainty into confidence.

Shape: utility bar → nav → hero (process-led, not catalogue-led) → services grid → process (1·2·3·4 steps) → materials & finishes gallery → past projects → quote form (the heart) → about → FAQ → reviews → footer. Massive underserved UK segment (~150k+ businesses) — enquiry-led but transactional in feel.
