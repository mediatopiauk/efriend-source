— The Story —

An old pub. A new fire. The same idea since 1847.

The Forge & Crown has stood on Princess Victoria Street since 1847 — first as a coaching inn, later as a working-man's pub, then quietly forgotten. We took it on in 2019, tore out the carpet, and built a proper wood-fired hearth where the dartboard used to be.

We work with the people who grow, raise and catch what ends up on the plate — Pete the smoker in St Werburghs, the Trethowans at Park Farm, the boats out of Brixham. We bake our own bread, brew our own pale ale, and pull pints for the regulars who still call this their local.

It's a pub first. The food just happens to be very, very good.

— Tom & Ellie FairfaxChef-owners
