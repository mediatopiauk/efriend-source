Shop FAQ

How long does shipping take?

UK orders ship the same day if placed by 2pm — typically arrive 1–2 working days via tracked Royal Mail. International orders 5–10 days depending on destination.

What's your returns policy?

30 days no-questions returns on unused product. Opened soaps can be returned if you're not happy with the scent — we donate them to local shelters.

Do you offer trade pricing?

Yes — barbershops, hotels and approved retailers get 20–35% off depending on volume. Apply for a trade account using the form at the top of any page.

Are products vegan / cruelty-free?

All soaps are cruelty-free. The Senate, Lyceum and Hadrian are vegan. Other pucks use ethically-sourced tallow — full ingredients on every product page.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal. Trade accounts can pay on 30 or 60 day invoice terms.
