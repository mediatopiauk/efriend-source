01 — Our craft

Quietest salon you've ever stood in.

Brightside was built around a simple constraint: one dog in the room at a time. No background howling, no waiting in a crate, no shouted conversations. Just you, your dog, and Em — for as long as the appointment needs.

It's a slower way to groom. Most dogs who've been turned away elsewhere settle within ten minutes.
