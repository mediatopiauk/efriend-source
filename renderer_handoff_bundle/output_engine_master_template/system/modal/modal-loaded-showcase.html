Wayfinding · 24 panels · 7 sites

Ironbridge Gorge Museums

Oct 2024Delivered 7 weeks1 wk under £18,400ex VAT

The brief

Replace 24 weather-beaten wayfinding panels across 7 museum sites in the Severn valley. The previous supplier had used standard signboard — laminated and laser-cut — and the local climate had eaten through it in under three years. Budget pressure: roughly 30% under what they'd paid last time. Lead time: 8 weeks from sign-off.

What we did

3mm Dibond aluminium composite, CNC-routed and UV-printed in our Bridgnorth workshop. Subsurface-mounted to the existing posts using bespoke brackets we fabricated in-house. Every panel was dry-fit at the workshop, photographed against a reference grid, and shipped in numbered crates with matching install sheets.

The outcome

All 24 panels installed across two install weekends. The museum reports zero damage 14 months in, despite a brutal winter and one storm that uprooted a tree six metres from one of the sites. We've since had three referrals from neighbouring heritage attractions in Shropshire and Worcestershire.
